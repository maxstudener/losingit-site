Set your starting weight and your goal, and Losing It gives you one bubble for every pound in between. Log a weigh-in and watch them pop as the scale comes down. Track calories too, if you like: search thousands of foods and brands (everyday foods work even offline), scan a barcode or add a food by hand, bring in recipes from Safari, recipe apps, screenshots or cookbook pages, turn a receipt into a meal, and set an optional daily target you choose. Follow your progress with charts and a full history, keep progress photos with a before-and-after compare, earn badges and milestones along the way, and look back on your journey in story-style recaps on the Glow tab. Daily affirmations and an optional Glow Coach, which writes short pep talks on your iPhone on devices with Apple Intelligence, keep the encouragement coming, and Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets keep your progress in view. Your journal and food log are private: they stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud.

One-time purchase. No account, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads, no tracking.
