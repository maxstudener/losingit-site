Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026 · Updated November 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Losing It app for iPhone (“the app”) handles your information. Losing It is made and sold by Powerup Gaming LLC (“we”, “us”).

The short version

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect, see, sell or share any of your personal data.
  • There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking of any kind.
  • Your journal and food log live on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database, which only you can access.
  • When you scan or type a food barcode, only that barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look the product up. Nothing about you goes with it.

What the app keeps, and where

To work, the app stores what you enter:

  • Your journey: the name you enter, your starting weight, goal weight and start date, and your daily calorie target if you set one.
  • Weigh-ins: each weight with its date, and any mood or note you add.
  • Progress photos: the photos you add, with their date, any caption you write, and the weight from the closest weigh-in.
  • Food log: each food you log, with its name, brand, calories, servings, meal and date, and its barcode if it came from a scan or a typed barcode.
  • Settings: your reminder time, favorite affirmations and the Glow Coach switch.

On your iPhone

All of this is stored in the app’s own storage on your device.

In your iCloud, if iCloud is on

If you are signed in to iCloud, the app syncs your journey, weigh-ins, photos and food log through Apple’s CloudKit to the private database of your own iCloud account, so they can appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. That private database belongs to you: only you can access it, and we cannot see it. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. Your settings (reminder time, favorite affirmations and the Glow Coach switch) are not synced and stay on each device.

Nothing comes to us

We do not run any servers for the app. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or other third-party code. Apart from your own iCloud and the barcode lookups described under Food lookups, the app does not connect to anything, and it never sends your information to us or to anyone else. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports for the app. That sharing is run by Apple under your iOS settings, not by code in the app, and it does not include what you write in your journal.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only for progress photos and for scanning food barcodes. Your photo library is used only for progress photos and recap cards. Both are used only when you choose to:

  • Camera: iOS asks for your permission the first time you tap the camera button to take a progress photo, or the first time you scan a barcode. While you scan, the camera view is read on your iPhone to find the barcode. No picture is saved or sent. Only the barcode number is used, as described under Food lookups.
  • Photo library: the app uses Apple’s photo picker, so it receives only the photos you pick. It does not get access to the rest of your library.
  • Saving to Photos: if you use the share button to save a progress photo or recap card to your photo library, iOS asks for permission to add it. That permission only lets the app add images; it cannot read your library.

The app saves a resized copy of each photo in your journal and reads the date the photo was taken so it lands in the right place on your timeline. The copy it keeps does not include the photo’s location or other camera details. Photos stay on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud database.

Food lookups

When you scan or type a barcode on the Food tab, the app sends only that barcode number, over an encrypted HTTPS connection, to Open Food Facts, an open, non-profit food database. It asks for that product’s name, brand, serving size and calories, and nothing else.

  • What is not sent: no name, weight, photos, food log, account or device identifier. The request names the app, not you.
  • What Open Food Facts can see: like any website you visit, its servers can see the internet (IP) address the request comes from. How Open Food Facts handles that is covered by the Open Food Facts privacy policy.
  • When nothing is sent: if you have logged that barcode before, the app uses your own entry and does not look it up again. Foods you add by hand and foods you add again from Recent are never sent anywhere.

The foods you log are stored like your weigh-ins: on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in your private iCloud database. Product information comes from Open Food Facts and is available under the Open Database License.

Glow Coach

Glow Coach is an optional feature that writes short, encouraging notes. It uses Apple’s on-device language model (part of Apple Intelligence) and runs entirely on your iPhone. The notes are written from a few facts about your journey, such as your first name, pounds lost, pounds to go and your weigh-in streak. Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. You can turn Glow Coach off at any time in the app’s Settings. When it is off or unavailable on your device, the app shows its built-in encouragement instead.

Widgets

Losing It’s Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small summary that the app saves on your device for them, such as your first name, your progress, your next bubbles, your streak and today’s Glow Coach note. This summary stays on your device. Keep in mind that a Lock Screen widget can be seen by anyone who can see your Lock Screen.

Reminders

If you turn on daily weigh-in reminders, iOS asks for your permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself; we do not send them and never learn when or whether you weigh in.

Sharing

If you choose to share a recap card or a progress photo with the share button, iOS sends it to the app or person you pick. What happens next is up to you and that service. The app never shares anything on its own.

Deleting your data

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You are in full control:

  • In the app: tap the gear icon on the Journey tab to open Settings, then tap Erase everything & start fresh. This deletes your journey, weigh-ins, photos and food log, and clears the widgets, reminders and favorite affirmations. If iCloud sync is on, the erase also removes your journal from iCloud and from every device signed in to that iCloud account. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete the app: deleting Losing It removes everything it stored on that iPhone. A copy synced to iCloud stays there until you erase it in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • Manage iCloud data: in the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud. From there you can manage your iCloud storage and delete the data saved by Losing It, or turn off iCloud for the app in the list of apps that use iCloud.

Children

Losing It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect personal information at all.

Health disclaimer

Losing It is a personal journal for tracking and encouragement. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Glow Coach notes and affirmations are encouragement, not health guidance. The optional daily calorie target is a number you choose; the app does not recommend one, and calorie numbers from package labels or Open Food Facts may not be exact. Talk with a doctor or other qualified health professional before starting or changing a weight-loss plan, especially if you are pregnant, have a health condition, or have a history of disordered eating.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above. If a future version of the app ever handles your information differently, this page will be updated before that version is released.
