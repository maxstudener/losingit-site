Frequently asked questions

How does iCloud sync work, and why isn’t my data syncing?

Losing It syncs your journey, weigh-ins, photos and food log automatically through your own private iCloud. For sync to work:

  • Be signed in to iCloud in the Settings app, with iCloud Drive turned on.
  • Use the same Apple Account on each device.
  • Make sure Losing It is allowed to use iCloud (Settings → your name → iCloud, in the list of apps using iCloud).
  • Be connected to the internet.

Changes can take a few minutes to show up on your other devices, and photos can take longer. Opening the app on both devices while on Wi-Fi helps. You can check the sync status in Losing It under Settings → iCloud.

Your reminder time, favorite affirmations and the Glow Coach switch are saved per device and don’t sync.

I got a new iPhone. How do I get my journey back?
  1. Sign in to the new iPhone with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud and iCloud Drive.
  2. Download Losing It from the App Store. Your purchase is tied to your Apple Account, so you won’t pay again.
  3. Open the app. It shows “Looking for your journey in iCloud” while your journey comes over. Photos may take a little longer to arrive.

If the welcome screen appears instead, your journey may not have arrived yet. Close the app, wait a few minutes on Wi-Fi, and open it again. If you already set up a new journey before the old one arrived, the app keeps your original journey once both are synced, along with any weigh-ins you logged in the meantime.

How do I add a widget?

Home Screen: touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Search for Losing It and pick Pound Bubbles (small, medium or large) or Daily Affirmation (small or medium), then tap Add Widget.

Lock Screen: touch and hold your Lock Screen, tap Customize, choose the Lock Screen, tap the widget area, and pick Glow Progress from Losing It.

Widgets update when you log a weigh-in. If one looks out of date, open the app for a moment. Until you’ve set up your journey in the app, widgets say “Open Losing It to begin.”

Why don’t I see Glow Coach?

Glow Coach writes its notes with Apple’s on-device model, so it needs iOS 26 or later on an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on (Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri). Right after you turn Apple Intelligence on, the model may still be downloading, so give it a little time.

You can check Glow Coach’s status in Losing It under Settings → Glow Coach, and switch it off there with Personal pep talks. When Glow Coach isn’t available, Losing It shows its built-in encouragement and affirmations instead. Everything else works the same.

How do I edit or delete a weigh-in?

Go to the Progress tab and tap a weigh-in under Recent weigh-ins. You can change the weight, date, mood or note and tap Save changes, or tap Delete this weigh-in.

For older entries, tap See all (shown once you have more than five weigh-ins). Tap any entry to edit it, or swipe left on it for Edit and Delete.

How do I change my goal?

On the Journey tab, tap the gear icon at the top right to open Settings. Under Your journey you can change your goal weight, as well as your starting weight, name and start date. Your pound bubbles update to match.

Your goal needs to be far enough below your starting weight to leave at least one pound bubble to pop. If it isn’t, Settings tells you and keeps your previous weights until it is.

How do I scan a food barcode?

On the Food tab, tap Add food, then Scan a barcode. The first time, iOS asks for permission to use the camera. Then point your iPhone at the barcode on the package and the app reads it by itself.

  • Find good light, and avoid glare on shiny wrappers.
  • Hold steady a few inches away, with the whole barcode in view. Pinch to zoom in on a small one.
  • Flatten curved or crumpled packaging as best you can.

If it still won’t read, tap Type it instead and type the numbers printed under the stripes (8 to 14 digits), then tap Look it up. You can also choose Type a barcode straight from the Add food screen.

If camera access is off, turn it on in the iOS Settings app under Apps → Losing It → Camera, or just type the numbers. Looking up a barcode needs an internet connection.

A product wasn’t found, or it has no calories.

Product details come from Open Food Facts, an open food database filled in by volunteers, so some products are missing or incomplete.

  • Not found: the app opens a short form so you can add it by hand with a name and the calories from the label. It keeps the barcode, so the next time you scan that product the app fills it in from your own log.
  • No calories listed: the app shows what it found and asks you to type in the number from the label.
  • Calories don’t match your package: tap Adjust the details before you add it, or tap the food later on the Food tab to edit it.
  • Lookup didn’t go through: check your connection and tap Try again, or tap Add it yourself.

You can always skip the barcode entirely: tap Add food, then Add it yourself.

How do I set, change or turn off my calorie target?

A daily calorie target is completely optional, and it’s off until you set one. Without it, the Food tab is a simple log.

  • Set or change it: on the Food tab, tap Set a daily target (or Change target). You can also open Settings (the gear icon on the Journey tab) and tap Daily calorie target under Food. Type your number and tap Save my target.
  • Turn it off: open the same screen and tap Turn off my target.

The target can be anything from 1,200 to 5,000 calories a day. Losing It never suggests a number, so please talk with your doctor about what’s right for you. If a day goes over your target, the app simply says that’s okay. Tomorrow is a fresh page.

How do I reset and start over?

Open Settings (the gear icon on the Journey tab), scroll to the bottom and tap Erase everything & start fresh, then confirm. This deletes your journey, weigh-ins, photos and food log and clears the widgets and reminders.

If iCloud sync is on, this also erases your journal from iCloud and from every device signed in to that iCloud account. It can’t be undone.

To remove Losing It from one iPhone only, delete the app from that iPhone. Your iCloud copy stays and comes back if you reinstall.

Can I use kilograms?

Not yet. Losing It currently tracks weight in pounds only.

My weigh-in reminders aren’t showing up.

Turn on Daily weigh-in reminder in the app’s Settings and pick a time. If notifications are off for Losing It, Settings in the app lets you know; turn them on in the iOS Settings app under Notifications → Losing It.

Is there anything else to buy?

No. Losing It is a one-time purchase with no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads and no account.

Is Losing It medical advice?

No. Losing It is a personal journal for tracking and encouragement. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and Glow Coach notes and affirmations are encouragement, not health guidance.

The food log is the same. It doesn’t give diet or nutrition advice, the calorie target is a number you choose rather than a recommendation, and calorie numbers from labels or Open Food Facts may not be exact.

Talk with a doctor or other qualified health professional before starting or changing a weight-loss plan, especially if you are pregnant, have a health condition, or have a history of disordered eating.
